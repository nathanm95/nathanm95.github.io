Featured case study

Salesforce Build for a Property Housing Services Provider

A housing partner based in Medway, Kent, providing accommodation on behalf of local authorities across the South-East of England — 1,400+ properties, 25+ local authority relationships. I led the build that moved their entire operation — previously split across two third-party systems and a wall of spreadsheets — onto a single Salesforce org, largely as a solo build, with regular presentations into their senior leadership (PAM) meetings.

3 → 1systems consolidated onto one platform
1,400+properties under management
25+local authority relationships served
100+staff using the org day to day

Consolidating three systems into one

Property, booking and tenancy data lived across two legacy housing-sector systems plus an assortment of spreadsheets. I designed the migration and data model to bring all of it into a single Salesforce org — including untangling duplicate council records (e.g. a council and its children's-services department that were really the same organisation) into a clean parent/child account hierarchy that still holds up for reporting today.

End-to-end booking lifecycle

Modelled the full booking process as a staged Opportunity pipeline — from a daily "available void" list, through council selection and nightly-rate agreement, guest agreement sign-off, utilities handover, move-in, and eventually sign-out — so every team always knows exactly where a booking sits.

Duty-of-care safeguarding flags

Built safeguarding indicators directly onto client records — tracked with dates — that surface as banners on the booking and property pages, so any staff member visiting a property is immediately aware of relevant risk information for that tenant.

Dynamic activity timeline

Replaced scattered email and note-taking with a single screen-flow driven events log that can attach to any record — booking, client or property — pre-filling context automatically and routing notifications to the right team via a configurable custom setting, no code changes required to add a new team.

AI-assisted repairs triage

Built a public web-to-case flow for tenants to report repairs, wired into a third-party AI diagnostic tool that walks the tenant through troubleshooting before a case ever reaches the maintenance team — cutting unnecessary site visits and giving the team a full transcript when a real repair is needed.

Certification compliance automation

Scheduled flows automatically generate the next compliance certificate (gas safety and others) as soon as one is completed, with reminder windows ahead of the due date — turning a spreadsheet-based compliance risk into a self-managing process.

Council self-service portal

Delivered an Experience Cloud portal giving local authority partners direct visibility into their booked properties, certifications and standards documentation — work councils previously had to phone in for. A genuine differentiator against other housing providers in the market.

Void board dashboard

The most extensive dashboard in the build: a live view of every vacant property, days-void, and estimated revenue impact. It's the business development team's primary tool for driving voids toward zero — displayed on-screen in their office every day.

Field mobile app for van teams

Set up and custom-configured the Salesforce mobile app with tailored layouts for maintenance staff working from tablets in the field, including a driver-facing job-completion workflow.
